# Clinical Validation Framework & Research Protocol - TheraVox AI

## 1. Executive Summary
TheraVox AI combines Speech Emotion Recognition (SER), Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) interactive modules, and real-time distress detection. This framework establishes clinical study protocols for efficacy validation in collaboration with licensed mental health institutions.

## 2. Primary Clinical Hypotheses
1. **P1 (Anxiety & Stress Mitigation)**: Users completing 3 sessions of CBT Thought Restructuring show a statistically significant reduction in GAD-7 anxiety scores over 14 days.
2. **P2 (Crisis Detection Accuracy)**: The hybrid rules-and-lexicon crisis engine achieves >95% recall on high-risk ideation signals with minimal false positives.

## 3. Ethics & Informed Consent Protocol
- **Anonymized Data Pipeline**: Data exported for study analysis replaces all identifiers with 16-character SHA-256 hashes.
- **Opt-In Consent**: Participation in clinical research trials requires explicit digital opt-in consent from users.
