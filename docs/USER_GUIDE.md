## 1. Getting Started & Account Creation

### Registering Your Account
1. Open the TheraVox AI Web App in your browser or install it as a PWA on mobile.
2. Click **Get Started** or **Register**.
3. Enter your full name, email address, and secure password, or select **Continue with Google** / **Continue with GitHub** for quick OAuth login.

### Language Selection
1. Use the globe icon dropdown in the top header.
2. Select your preferred language: **English (EN)**, **हिंदी (HI)**, or **Español (ES)**. The entire user interface will instantly update.

## 2. Multimodal Emotion Detection

TheraVox AI analyzes your emotional state across 3 distinct channels:

### A. Text Analysis
1. Navigate to **Emotion Analysis -> Text**.
2. Type or paste your journal entry or thoughts into the text area.
3. Click **Analyze Text Emotion**.
4. The system will display your primary detected emotion (e.g., Happy, Sad, Anxious), confidence score, and radar chart breakdown.

### B. Audio Analysis (Voice Emotion)
1. Navigate to **Emotion Analysis -> Audio**.
2. Either upload an audio file (`.wav`, `.mp3`, `.ogg`) or click **Start Recording** to record your voice directly in the browser.
3. Click **Analyze Speech Emotion** to process acoustic pitch and vocal tone.

### C. Facial Video Analysis
1. Navigate to **Emotion Analysis -> Vision**.
2. Grant camera permissions.
3. Point your camera at your face in a well-lit area. Real-time facial expression smoothing will evaluate micro-expressions.

## 3. Mindful AI Companion Chat

1. Navigate to **Mindful Companion Chat**.
2. Start a new conversation with **MindfulMind AI**.
3. Share your thoughts freely. The AI companion uses empathetic therapeutic dialogue techniques to help you process emotions.
4. Click **Generate Summary** at the end of a conversation to receive AI-curated key themes, action items, and your session mood arc.

## 4. Therapeutic Programs & CBT Modules

1. Navigate to **Therapy Hub**.

## 6. Crisis Escalation & Emergency Contacts

### Setting Emergency Contacts
1. Go to **Settings / Profile**.
2. Scroll to **Emergency Contacts (SMS Crisis Escalation)**.
3. Enter your contact's name, phone number (with country code, e.g., `+1...`), and relationship.
4. Click **Add Contact**.

### How Crisis Escalation Works
If the real-time safety scanner detects severe distress signals during text analysis or AI chat:
- An immediate crisis resource pop-up will offer national helpline numbers (988, Vandrevala Foundation, etc.).
- An automated urgent **SMS Alert** will be sent to your primary emergency contact.
- An encrypted escalation report will be emailed to clinical administrators.

## 7. Therapist Portal (Professional Instructions)

### For Licensed Mental Health Practitioners

#### 1. Obtaining Therapist Role
Log in with your clinician account. If assigned a therapist role, the **Therapist Portal** navigation item will appear in your sidebar.

#### 2. Linking Clients
1. In the Therapist Portal, click **Generate Client Invite Code**.
2. A unique 6-character code (e.g., `AB12CD`) will be generated.
3. Share this code with your patient.
4. Ask your patient to enter the invitation code under **Settings -> Link Therapist**.

#### 3. Patient Monitoring & Data Privacy
- Once linked, you can view your client list and status in the portal.
- **Consent Rule**: Patient mood trends and crisis alert histories are viewable **only** when the client maintains active consent (`Consent: Shared ✅`). Clients can revoke data sharing at any time.

## 8. Data Export & Privacy Settings

### Downloading Your Complete Archive (Data Portability)
1. Go to **Profile / Settings**.
2. Under **Export Your Data**, click **Download Archive**.
3. The system will compile and download a structured `.zip` archive containing:
   - `profile.json`
   - `wellness_entries.json`
   - `chat_sessions.json`
   - `crisis_alerts.json`
   - `feedback_history.json`
4. *Note: Data export is rate-limited to once every 24 hours.*

### Push Notification Time Settings
1. In **Profile / Settings**, click **Enable Web Push**.
2. Set your preferred daily check-in reminder time (e.g., `20:00`). You will receive a daily gentle reminder.
